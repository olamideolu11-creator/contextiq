The problem

First-pass contract review is still too manual.

Lawyers review contracts based on context: urgency, leverage, value, risk tolerance, and counterparty relationship. But most workflows still treat every contract the same.

⏱

Slow review cycles

Legal teams repeatedly scan the same clauses across NDAs, MSAs, vendor agreements, SaaS contracts, and customer contracts.

🧩

Missing deal context

Business teams often send contracts without clear urgency, leverage, deal value, or acceptable risk level.

⚠️

Generic AI output

Many tools flag clauses without explaining why they matter in the specific negotiation, creating noise instead of trust.
